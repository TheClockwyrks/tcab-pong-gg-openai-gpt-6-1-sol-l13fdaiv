# Carom

Two paddles. One ball. No straight answers. Carom is a fast paddle duel where the angle of your return is only the beginning. Take on the CPU in Solo or challenge a friend across one keyboard in local Versus.

The opening serve gives you a second to settle in. Meet the ball near the center of your paddle for a clean return, or catch it near the tip to send it wide. Every paddle contact makes the rally a little faster, until a comfortable exchange becomes a test of instinct.

Move through the strike and the ball bends away with spin. Its bright trail traces the curve, giving you just enough information to read the next shot. The orange obstacles sway and turn between you and the other side: a safe lane can close, and an awkward return can become a beautiful bank shot.

First to eleven wins, but you need a two-point lead. At match point, the choice is yours: hold the line and trust your timing, or put a little spin on it and find an angle your opponent never saw coming.
